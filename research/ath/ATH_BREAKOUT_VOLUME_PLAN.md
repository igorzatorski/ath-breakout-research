# Breakout-day relative volume: twenty exploratory variants

Question: does high relative volume on the original ATH breakout day improve annual ATH + common 6-1/top20 returns? This is distinct from pre-breakout volume dry-up. Do not combine either volume or price-range filter in this task.

Freeze reference lengths L = 5, 10, 21, 42, 63 scheduled NYSE sessions. Signal-day relative volume = comparable volume[t] / mean(comparable volume[t-L:t-1]). Comparable volume = raw volume * positive finite share_adjustment_factor, following the existing CRSP layer. The denominator excludes the signal day. Filters: relative volume >=1.0, >=1.5, >=2.0, >=3.0 (inclusive comparison tolerance 1e-12). Exactly twenty cumulative threshold variants. Signal-day volume is known after the close, before the original next-open entry. Do not imply exact real-time consolidated availability or modeled fills/costs.

Require finite nonnegative volume on t and every reference session, finite positive factors, and strictly positive prior mean. A valid zero current volume gives ratio zero; a zero prior mean gives an undefined ratio, not imputation. No forward fill. Report per-window missingness, zero denominator and zero current volume; compare original 23,712 annual pairs with common observed support across all five references. Record all 25,250 original signals before annual outcome exclusions. All original returns, ranks, universe, execution, 252-session endpoint, distributions, terminal cash and nonoverlap flags remain frozen.

Report all twenty counts, stock/SPY/excess means, common-reference improvements, stock/excess medians, loss frequency, skewness, equal-company/date weighting and >=100/200/300% winner frequency/retention. Include fixed periods, annual cohorts and original nonoverlap sensitivity. Save adjacent reference/threshold contrasts. Also show mutually exclusive relative-volume bands <1, [1,1.5), [1.5,2), [2,3), >=3 for each reference as descriptive accounting, not an extra inferential family. Full bands preserve low-volume cases and avoid mistaking nested threshold outcomes for independent groups.

Approximate joint crossed event-company/circular date-block bootstrap: 2,000 draws, seed 20261016, blocks 252/504. Primary family: twenty full-period all-event improvements. Same weights for all cells and reference, pointwise and centered max-absolute-deviation simultaneous twenty-family bands when at least 95% complete-family draws. Missing bounds remain explicit. Nonoverlap is sensitivity and period/band cuts are descriptive. This does not correct earlier hypotheses, omitted risk exposures, reporting differences or guarantee calibrated coverage. High volume need not be buying pressure, confirmation or causal information; news/earnings and other omitted conditions can differ. All history is inspected; no optimal rule, independent validation, factor alpha or portfolio result is established here.
