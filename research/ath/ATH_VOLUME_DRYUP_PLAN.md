# Pre-breakout volume contraction: fifteen exploratory variants

Question: does declining volume before the original ATH breakout improve annual ATH + common 6-1/top20 returns? Test volume alone, without the prior price-range contraction filter or breakout-day volume.

Freeze equal half-window H = 5, 10, 21, 42, 63 NYSE sessions, total windows 10, 20, 42, 84, 126. First half t-2H through t-H-1; recent half t-H through t-1. Volume = raw CRSP volume * positive finite share_adjustment_factor, consistent with existing crsp_adjustments.py. Each half uses the arithmetic mean of every scheduled session, excluding signal day. Ratio = recent mean / earlier mean. Exactly three cumulative cutoffs <=0.50, <=0.75, <=1.00 (comparison tolerance 1e-12); the last means non-increase rather than necessarily declining volume. This is share-volume contraction, not dollar-volume or turnover contraction.

Require all volumes finite and nonnegative and factors finite and positive. Valid zero daily volumes remain zeros. Earlier mean must be strictly positive; otherwise the ratio is undefined, not imputed. Recent mean zero with positive earlier mean is valid. No forward fill. Report missingness, zero-denominator counts and effects of common feature support across five half lengths against the unchanged original 23,712 annual pairs. Split-adjusted volume ratios are invariant to a common positive rescaling of the entire window; no future return or signal-day volume is used.

Preserve historical universe, momentum ranks/top20, ATH signal, next-open entry, 252-session return, distributions, terminal cash and original nonoverlap flags. Record all 25,250 source signals before outcome exclusions. No absolute width, proximity, volatility contraction, combination, exit, ranking or portfolio condition is added. No threshold search extension after results.

Report all fifteen counts, stock/SPY/excess means, common-base improvements, medians, losses, skewness and >=100/200/300% winner frequencies/retention; equal-company/date weighting, fixed historical and annual cuts and original nonoverlap sensitivity. Price trends, share issuance/buybacks and reporting changes are not separately controlled by this volume measure; it does not identify buying/selling pressure or a causal mechanism.

Joint approximate crossed event-company/circular date-block bootstrap: 2,000 draws, seed 20261016, blocks 252/504. Pointwise bands and centered max-absolute-deviation simultaneous fifteen-improvement bands require 95% complete-family support; otherwise preserve missing bounds without deleting cells. Primary family is full-period all-event improvements. Other cuts are sensitivity/descriptive. This does not correct earlier inspected trials, omitted factors or guarantee calibrated coverage. All historical data are exploratory, not untouched validation. No volume-on-breakout, execution/exit or portfolio backtest is run.
