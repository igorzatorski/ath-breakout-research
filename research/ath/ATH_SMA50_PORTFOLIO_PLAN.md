# First fresh-ATH portfolio: SMA50 exits and two weighting policies

ATH28, authorized2026-10-17. First exploratory actual-capital backtest of the user's requested20-company portfolio, not an event-mean study. USD100,000 initial cash, fractional shares, no leverage/taxes, zero cash interest. Simulate all NYSE sessions1994-01-03 through2026-06-30 using frozen local raw CRSP and matched SPY history. All inspected data remains development history; no independent-validation or confirmed-alpha claim.

Signals: all25,250 pre-outcome ATH + common6-1 market-momentum/top20 source events from ATH27, including signals without mature annual outcomes and dates with fewer than5 candidates. Do not use annual returns, primary ranking evaluation masks or eventual holding outcomes to select entries. Rank each signal date by the already fixed MV score, then original6-1 momentum descending, PERMNO ascending. Signal is valid only for the next scheduled NYSE opening; no stale signal pool or monthly replacement list. Maximum20 distinct PERMCOs, one security per company. Skip already held companies and exhausted capacity. A missing observed opening price invalidates that attempted entry, logged rather than imputed. A final-date signal cannot execute beyond end of data.

Exit: observe split-adjusted close below arithmetic SMA50 including current close, with all50 scheduled positive closes required. Create an order only after that close; execute at next observed tradable opening, with missing scheduled opens explicitly deferred/logged. No sell solely for rank deterioration or universe removal. No take-profit, ATR stop,252-session holding cap or monthly security replacement. New positions may be entered on fresh signals while slots are available.

Policies, fixed before outcomes:
1. drift: new purchase targets5% of current opening NAV; existing share quantities drift. No monthly trimming or topping up. Available cash may limit the last purchase; never borrow. Thus5% is an entry target, not a permanent cap.
2. monthly_equal: same entries/exits; after each month-end close request a weight reset at the next tradable opening, including eligible fresh additions in a single net rebalance. Every current position targets5% of post-cost NAV; fewer than20 names leave unfilled slots as cash. Solve transaction-cost-aware target dollars, sell overweight positions before buying. Defer the reset if a held position cannot trade at that opening. Do not sell a name outright merely because it falls in rank.

Run both policies at10bps one-way proportional cost (primary) and0bps (gross reference), not a cost-parameter optimization. Charge actual trade notional; terminal corporate cash settlement has no market-trade fee. Target near100% investment only when fresh eligible candidates and cash permit. Never buy outside the rules or lever remaining names to manufacture full exposure. Dividends may leave residual cash, used for subsequent fresh entries/rebalances.

Execution/accounting: use split-adjusted price units (raw price / positive cumulative price factor), preserve economic shares through splits, credit ordinary+nonordinary ex-date cash only to overnight holdings before opening trades. CIZ distribution amounts use pre-event share units: divide by current cumulative price factor times period price factor on simultaneous split/distribution dates. New ex-date buyers do not receive that day's distribution. Mark holdings at actual close. On regular held overnight observations with an observed matching previous source close, reconcile (adjusted_close + adjusted_distribution)/previous_adjusted_close -1 to CRSP daily total_return within1e-5 absolute return; fail on material unexplained discrepancies. Following a missing source close, flag/skip this comparison rather than comparing a source return against a stale valuation with different endpoints. Ordinary cash dividends are not also compounded through CRSP total returns. Cash-ledger treatment deliberately differs from automatic stock-level reinvestment.

CIZ terminal Y daily return already includes terminal effects: terminal nonordinary cash payouts are part of that settlement, not a second opening dividend. On Y rows credit only a separately valid ordinary ex-date dividend (zero ordinary amount needs no missing terminal factor), then settle stock once at close using prior adjusted close*(1+total_return) minus that separately credited ordinary dividend per adjusted unit. Never multiply by another delisting return. A pending opening sale may execute before terminal settlement if a genuine opening price exists. Corporate proceeds are unavailable for purchases until after settlement. Apply the same per-unit terminal payoff to remaining shares; no future terminal flag is used to reject an otherwise executable opening entry.

Missing held observations: never invent executable prices. Pending exits remain pending if opening quotes are unavailable. If a held close/50-day SMA is missing, keep last observable valuation, flag stale marking and suspend a new SMA decision rather than zero-fill a return or silently remove the stock; disclose all such cases. If stale marks persist at the end, results are provisional. Missing distributions on an observed held row or missing terminal return block the run. All NAV, cash, cost, unique-company and capacity invariants must hold.

Benchmark: SPY total-return buy-and-hold over identical dates, entry at first simulation open, adjusted-close reinvestment thereafter; charge the same one-time entry cost in the10bps comparison.
