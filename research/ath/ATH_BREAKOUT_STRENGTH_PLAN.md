# Breakout magnitude relative to strictly prior ATR

ATH24 exploratory standalone entry-feature study on the frozen ATH + common 6-1 momentum/top20 sample. Question: do larger close-confirmed overshoots of the prior ATH predict different subsequent annual outcomes? Keep stock selection distinct from allocation to cash; no market regime or portfolio allocation is tested.

Feature at t = (split-adjusted signal close - frozen prior all-history HIGH bound) / ATR20(t-1). ATR here is the simple arithmetic mean of the twenty true ranges for scheduled sessions t-20 through t-1, NOT Wilder smoothing. TR(s) = max(high(s)-low(s), abs(high(s)-close(s-1)), abs(low(s)-close(s-1))). High, low and close all use price / positive price_adjustment_factor. Require finite positive OHLC with low <= close <= high, complete scheduled dates and previous close. A valid current TR requires the previous close, not the previous high/low. Require positive finite ATR. No signal-day high/low enters the ATR denominator. Signal close is known before original next-open entry; the breakout-day price gain is excluded from outcomes.

Exactly five cumulative lower-bound filters: strength >=0.10, >=0.25, >=0.50, >=1.00, >=2.00 ATR. Comparisons allow absolute 1e-12 numerical tolerance. Six descriptive disjoint bands use the identical tolerance: <0.10, 0.10-0.25, 0.25-0.50, 0.50-1.00, 1.00-2.00, >=2.00. No ATR-length search, upper-bound optimized filter, alternate numerator or combinations with previous filters after observing results.
