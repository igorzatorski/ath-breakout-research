# Prior ATH proximity: sixteen frozen exploratory variants

Primary question: among frozen ATH + common 6-1/top20 events, does staying below but close to a fixed prior ATH before the eventual breakout improve annual returns?

Lengths L = 10, 21, 42, 63 scheduled NYSE sessions. Maximum depths d = 0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20. For signal session t, the window is t-L through t-1. Anchor A is the all-history split-adjusted HIGH bound known strictly before t-L, consistent with the original ATH definition and historical seed. Require all L adjusted closes observed and positive, anchor finite and positive, and every close in [A*(1-d), A]. The anchor stays fixed across the window. A closing breakout before t fails the upper condition; intraday highs above A do not by themselves fail a closing-price test. The signal at t remains the original true ATH against all highs through t-1.

No width filter, no new momentum rank/threshold, no changed execution or holding rule. Split-adjusted close uses original positive factor and price rules. Data missingness is explicit; no forward fill. Common feature support across all four lengths is primary, beside the full original 23,712 annual stock/SPY pairs. Preserve all source signals before outcome availability. Fixed original nonoverlap flags are never reselected within cells.

Report stock/SPY/excess means, median, counts, company/date weighting, losses, fixed periods and annual cohorts for every cell. Add four length-specific anchor-only accounting references (upper condition only, no lower floor) and exclusions for missing history, prior closing breakout and depth. These diagnose whether selection comes from no intermediate breakout rather than proximity; they are not extra optimized strategies. Cell improvements are reported against both unchanged ATH/momentum baseline and their anchor-only reference. No non-ATH control rematching in this experiment.

Approximate joint crossed event-company/date circular-block bootstrap: 2,000 draws, seed 20261016, blocks 252 and 504. Same weights for baseline, all 16 cells and four accounting references. Primary family: 16 full-period all-event improvements over the original baseline. Report pointwise level/improvement bands and a centered max-absolute-deviation simultaneous 16-family band only with at least 95% complete-family replicates. Missing/sparse support remains explicit; no cell is dropped to obtain a band. Other contrasts are descriptive sensitivity, not multiplicity-controlled alpha tests.

All data are previously inspected and this hypothesis is exploratory. No causal effect, optimal parameter, portfolio performance, factor alpha or untouched validation is established by these event means. Do not extend the grid after results or combine it with the earlier width filter in this task.
