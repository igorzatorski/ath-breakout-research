# Stock SMA position and trend alignment

Exploratory study ATH23 on frozen ATH + common 6-1 momentum/top20 events. Six filters only: close > SMA20, close > SMA50, close > SMA100, close > SMA200, SMA100 > SMA200, and SMA50 > SMA100 > SMA200. No SPY regime, slope, SMA20 alignment, extra threshold, exit, ranking or combined prior research filter is added.

Each SMA is the arithmetic mean of split-adjusted positive closing prices over exactly N scheduled NYSE sessions ending on the signal date, including the signal close. All observations are required; missing dates/prices remain missing. Signal-close information is available before original next-open entry. Use original close / positive price_adjustment_factor convention, not total-return wealth, and PERMNO identities. Strict comparisons treat differences <=1e-12 times the larger absolute operand as equality. There is no reset for splits or year boundaries and no future information.

An all-time closing breakout above the historical HIGH bound should mechanically exceed every observed prior comparable close. Thus price > SMA may be redundant; test coverage and failures rather than assume it selects a useful subset. The original ATH price bound and SMA conventions must be reconciled if observed failures occur. A missing SMA is not a failed valid trend.

Preserve 25,250 source events and original 23,712 annual stock/SPY pairs. Main reference uses common complete SMA20/50/100/200 and signal-close support, with explicit reconciliation to original population and separate natural support/pass/fail counts for each filter. If common support differs, do not attribute the baseline change to filtering. Save excluded IDs and their missing features in events.parquet. Keep momentum, universe, annual next-open returns, distributions, terminal cash and original company nonoverlap unchanged.

Report pass/fail counts, means of stock and same-window SPY, excess and improvement versus common reference, medians, losses, skew, >=100/200/300% winner frequency/retention, company/date weights, frozen periods and annual cohorts, and fixed original nonoverlap sensitivity. Also save complement outcomes to show what each condition removes. Hierarchy checks: 50>100>200 subset of 100>200. The SMA20/50/100/200 price filters need not generally be nested, even if they all pass here.

Use existing approximate crossed company/circular-date bootstrap, 2,000 draws, seed20261016, blocks252/504, pointwise and simultaneous-six improvement bands. Identical-to-reference filters have exactly zero improvement and zero contrast uncertainty; that does not establish a profitable new factor. Family bands address these six tests only, not the entire prior search. Retain uncertainty and independence limitations without binary significance-only conclusions.

Independent raw-window verification must reconstruct exact scheduled windows and direct arithmetic means without the production rolling implementation, reconcile frozen IDs/outcomes/ranks/nonoverlap, all summaries and hierarchy/complement counts. Focused tests must verify current-close inclusion, future exclusion, strict ties, missing scheduled observations and trend comparisons. Document results in English and preserve prior research.
