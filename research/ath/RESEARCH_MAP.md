# Research map: signal first

## Working hypothesis

A close above the highest earlier comparable daily HIGH may predict higher
forward total returns than the market and similar non-ATH stocks. This is the
ACTIVE question after the user reset the scope on 2026-10-15.

No portfolio, exits, technical filters or parameter tuning in this stage.
Protocol: [ATH_SIGNAL_STUDY_PLAN.md](ATH_SIGNAL_STUDY_PLAN.md).

## What has actually happened

1. Data/definition diagnostic: can we identify the event correctly? Found
   unresolved older-ATH provenance; groups are explicitly qualified.
2. Fixed-horizon baseline: what follows the signal if we do nothing clever?
   Compared 52W and ATH descriptively in 2024. No general signal winner found.
3. Path diagnostic: how do future winners and losers move? Many future winners
   retest the breakout level. These retrospective labels are not entry filters.
4. Exploratory exit-family pilot: three fixed exits on the same 2024 ATH entries.
   This tested a different hypothesis. Retain it but do not treat it as the
   current signal study or as a completed research/validation sequence.
5. CURRENT: unfiltered ATH forward-return study versus same-date SPY and five
   size/volatility-matched non-ATH controls, over available 1994-2026 history.
   Primary horizon 21 sessions; 5/63/126 descriptive. Known older-high and gap
   uncertainties restrict the primary sample; report that restriction openly.

## What is not yet done

The 2024 pilots are not evidence of generalization. First answer the current
signal question, assess predefined subperiods and resolve important data risks.
Only after reviewing that evidence consider a small, registered next hypothesis
with separate validation. Parameter optimization is not the current task.
Test broad stable ranges rather than choosing the single highest historical result.
Portfolio construction and risk constraints come after a defensible event model.

All previously inspected history is known data, not an untouched holdout.
Each trial stays in `experiments.csv`; negative trials are retained.
Do not merge stages by changing entries, exits, filters and sizing simultaneously.
