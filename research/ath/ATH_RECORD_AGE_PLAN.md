# Time since the prior ATH level was established

Exploratory question: does breaking an older all-history high differ from another recent record in the frozen ATH + common 6-1/top20 population?

Definition: age at signal t is the NYSE-session index difference between t and the prior session that first established the current prior ATH HIGH bound. Track every historical observation of the same PERMNO, not only qualified signals, momentum events, universe-member dates or close-confirmed breakouts. The comparable bound uses original adjusted HIGH and close fallback conventions. A strictly larger historical bound resets the record date; an equal retouch does not. Capture age before applying t's new bound, so no age zero from the signal itself. Security identities and original ATH definition remain unchanged.

Initialize from the original pre-1993 maximum high/close seed. The seed has no record-creation date: do not pretend its last observation date is the record date. For seeded levels not surpassed since 1993, the creation date lies before observed history and exact elapsed age is unknown; save exact age as missing and a conservative lower bound from December 31, 1992. Qualified signals start in 1994; the bound is enough to classify such cases in the oldest >=252-session group. Unknown cases that could straddle a requested cutoff must be reported/excluded from common classified support, never imputed as exact dates. Describe this as an unknown earlier date / lower-bounded elapsed age, not a measured exact duration.

Exactly five cumulative filters: age >=5, >=21, >=63, >=126, >=252 sessions. Descriptive disjoint bands 1-4, 5-20, 21-62, 63-125, 126-251, >=252. Censored cases are assigned only when the entire possible age range lies in the final band; exact-age statistics never use the bound as an exact value. No upper-bound age, alternate record definition or threshold search after results.

Preserve all 25,250 source ATH/momentum signals before annual exclusions; original annual stock/SPY reference is 23,712. Report exact/censored/unclassified availability and common classified support, against original reference. Keep momentum, historical universe, next-open entry, 252-session return, distributions, terminal cash and original nonoverlap unchanged. Do not combine volume, consolidation, price closing location, exit, ranking or portfolio rules.

Report all five counts, stock/SPY/excess means, change versus classified base, stock/excess medians, losses, skewness, >=100/200/300% winner frequencies/retention, company/date weighting, fixed periods and annual cohorts and original nonoverlap sensitivity. Joint approximate event-company/circular date-block bootstrap: 2,000 draws, seed 20261016, blocks 252/504, pointwise and centered max-absolute-deviation simultaneous-five improvement bands with >=95% complete-family support. Primary family is full-period all-event improvements; bands and periods are descriptive. Prior research choices and calibration limitations remain.

Keep a separate interpretation note: excess over SPY is not the same estimand as filter improvement over ATH/momentum base. Confidence intervals concern uncertainty in an estimated average/contrast, not the range of individual trades or years. Lack of significance does not establish no effect; a positive historical mean does not establish a positive future effect. Repeated/overlapping events, common historical episodes and right-tail concentration reduce independent information. The existing crossed bootstrap is an approximate diagnostic, not a guaranteed 95% test. Do not alter intervals or confidence levels to obtain a desired conclusion.
