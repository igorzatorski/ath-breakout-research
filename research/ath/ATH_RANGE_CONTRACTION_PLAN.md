# Range contraction before ATH: fifteen exploratory variants

Question: does a declining closing-price range before an ATH breakout improve the frozen annual ATH + 6-1/top20 event result? This tests changing range, separately from absolute width, proximity to ATH, volatility of returns, volume or exits.

Before outcome calculation, freeze half-window H = 5, 10, 21, 42, 63 scheduled NYSE sessions (total windows 10, 20, 42, 84, 126). Equal halves avoid comparing unequal opportunity to span a wide range. For signal t, first half is t-2H through t-H-1; second is t-H through t-1. In each half width = maximum split-adjusted close / minimum split-adjusted close - 1. Ratio = second width / first width. Filters: ratio <=0.50, <=0.75, <=1.00, inclusive with 1e-12 comparison tolerance. The 1.00 threshold is non-expansion and not necessarily strict contraction. No current-day or future price enters features.

Require every scheduled close finite and positive, factor finite and positive, and first width >1e-12. A flat/numerically zero first range makes the ratio undefined; do not impute an infinite or zero ratio. A zero second width with positive first width is valid contraction. Report per-window missingness and zero-denominator counts. Use common observed-ratio support across all five half lengths for primary comparisons; also disclose original 23,712 annual pairs, their unaltered +5.58 pp mean excess and any change from availability selection.

Exactly fifteen cumulative filters; no extra thresholds after results. Hold original common-momentum top20, next-open entry, 252-session endpoint, distributions, terminal cash and original nonoverlap flags fixed. No proximity, absolute width, volume, stop, ranking or portfolio rule is added. Source population before future-outcome exclusions stays recorded.

Report original/common support, counts, stock/SPY/excess mean, improvement relative to common-support base, median, losses, equal-company/date results, >=100/200/300% winner frequencies and retained shares, fixed early/middle/recent and annual cuts, original nonoverlap sensitivity and adjacent parameter contrasts. Range compression can occur without sideways consolidation or closeness to ATH; conclusions are conditional on this definition and the frozen momentum population.

Approximate joint crossed event-company/circular signal-date block bootstrap: 2,000 draws, seed 20261016, blocks 252/504. Joint weights for base and all fifteen cells. Pointwise bands plus a centered maximum absolute deviation simultaneous band for fifteen full-period all-event improvements, only with at least 95% complete-family draws. Missing support remains explicit. Nonoverlap is sensitivity and period cuts are descriptive. Bands do not correct for earlier research choices, omitted factors or guarantee calibrated coverage. All history is inspected, not untouched validation. Retain every result and input/code hash.
